# Community conduct

Be respectful and evidence-led. Explain assumptions; distinguish bugs from model limitations.
No harassment, discrimination, doxxing, unsolicited promotions or requests for private financial credentials.
Discuss synthetic examples rather than identifying household finances. Do not impersonate maintainers,
claim guaranteed investment outcomes or promote a token as affiliated with this project.

Maintainers may edit/remove violating content and restrict repeat offenders. Use the repository's available
private reporting route for sensitive concerns; do not post sensitive information publicly. A dedicated
private contact route must be verified before a broader community launch.
