## Technical thread

### 1

```text
Your portfolio is not your whole financial life.

That is the idea behind Open Family Office, a local-first open-source toolkit I built while connecting my Asset Management background with AI engineering.
```

### 2

```text
A household can be asset-rich and still have weak liquidity.

A home, a private business and locked pension assets may increase net worth without paying next month's bills.

So the model keeps net worth and spendable liquidity separate.
```

### 3

```text
One-off cash ≠ recurring income.

A business sale is especially easy to model incorrectly:
- cash arrives
- the business asset leaves
- future distributions may stop

Counting only the sale proceeds creates fake wealth.
```

### 4

```text
The AI architecture is intentionally boring where money is involved:

intent → Skill → canonical workflow → deterministic Python → explanation

The model interviews and explains. Python computes.
```

### 5

```text
MCP is optional, not the product.

The project works as a CLI + portable Agent Skills. MCP only adds a read-only interoperability layer for compatible hosts.
```
