## Welcome discussion

### Category

General

### Title

```text
Welcome to Open Family Office — what should a personal family office understand?
```

### Body

Open Family Office starts from one assumption:

> Your portfolio is not your whole financial life.

The project already models assets, liabilities, recurring and one-off income, liquidity, ownership, scenarios and a separately declared liquid investment sleeve.

The question I want this community to help answer is what belongs in the next layer.

What should an open, personal Family Office understand especially well?

- income durability / human capital;
- property;
- private businesses;
- super / pension;
- trusts and shared ownership;
- education funding;
- retirement;
- business exits;
- inheritance / windfalls;
- insurance;
- tax-aware assumptions;
- something else?

Please keep examples synthetic and avoid posting real account numbers or private financial records.

If you want to implement something, the `good first issue` and `help wanted` labels are the best place to start.

v0.5.1 packages the v0.5 experience and moves Open Family Office from a source-checkout-oriented project to an installable local toolkit.

Highlights:

- `ofo` CLI;
- private workspace onboarding;
- 10 user-facing portable Agent Skills;
- safe Agent-kit synchronization;
- offline dashboard export;
- optional read-only MCP;
- wheel smoke tests outside the source checkout.
