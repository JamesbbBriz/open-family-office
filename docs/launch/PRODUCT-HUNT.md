## Short description

```text
An open-source, local-first family wealth research toolkit for assets, debt, income quality, liquidity, ownership, scenarios and allocation research — with portable AI Agent Skills and optional read-only MCP.
```

## Maker comment

I built Open Family Office from a simple frustration: portfolio software can be sophisticated about securities while being surprisingly weak at modelling the household around them.

A household may have property, private businesses, pension/super, debt, variable income and future obligations. Those affect liquidity and risk capacity before you ever optimize an investment portfolio.

Open Family Office puts that broader model first.

The financial calculations are deterministic Python. AI Agent Skills provide the interview/research workflow. MCP is an optional read-only layer, not a requirement.

The project is MIT licensed and local-first. It does not log into banks, place trades or act as an autonomous adviser.

I’d love feedback on the modelling assumptions, onboarding and what a useful open-source “family office” should include next.
